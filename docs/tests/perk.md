# Perk: verification matrix

Boundaries follow [conventions › Testing](../conventions.md#testing). Who may get a perk is read from the caller's organization and from their standing in a program on every request, and a claim is counted under the perk's row lock, so `PerkTest` starts the full application over real HTTP against PostgreSQL, with only the SMTP server replaced. A perk for a program's applicants or shortlist needs submitted applications and released outcomes, so that scenario runs in `ReviewTest`, which has them.

| Contract | Regression it catches | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An operator writes a perk for an organization that exists and marks it Arranged by GenAI Fund: its title is one line of at most 80 characters, its addresses are `https` ones, a link perk has its link, two perks of one organization do not read the same whatever their case, a save sends the version its form read, and each change is in the audit trail; the organization reads it among its own and cannot change, publish or delete it; another organization finds no such perk; a perk a team got is archived and not deleted, one nobody got is deleted | A member writing as an operator, a perk of no organization, a link to `http` or `javascript:`, an operator overwriting another's change, a member rewording a perk under GenAI Fund's mark, or a deletion that loses what a team got | `PerkTest.anOperatorWritesAPerkForTheOrganizationThatGivesItAndOnlyOperatorsChangeOneGenAiFundArranged` |
| A member of an organization writes a draft perk and publishes it with no review; it goes only with its organization's solutions, which its form offers; a draft and an archived perk are on no page, in no catalogue and on no solution's card; a published one is, and a link for everyone is open to a visitor | Someone with no organization writing a perk, one company's perk on another's solution, a draft or archived perk read by the public, or a review standing between a partner and its own perk | `PerkTest.anOrganizationPublishesItsOwnPerkWithNoReviewAndItGoesWithItsOwnSolutionsOnly` |
| A shared code is never on a perk's page; someone signed out is asked to sign in, someone in no organization is refused with `PERK_ORGANIZATION_REQUIRED`; a member takes it for their team, which holds it once: a colleague reads the code, who took it and asks again for the same claim; the team finds it among its perks and the partner reads who got it | A code leaked to the public, a perk got with no organization, two claims for one team, or a team that cannot find its code again | `PerkTest.aCodeForMembersIsShownOnlyToATeamThatTakesItAndTheTeamHoldsItOnce` |
| A perk given as a list of codes is published once it holds codes, which only an operator loads, a code it holds already skipped; each team gets a code of its own in the order loaded until none is left, then `PERK_NO_PLACES_LEFT` and its page says it is full; each load is recorded with how many codes were new | Two teams given one code, a list published empty, a partner loading codes, or a load with no record | `PerkTest.aCodeListGivesEachTeamACodeOfItsOwnUntilTheLastIsTaken` |
| A request answers each required question and nothing it does not ask; with Require approval it waits, holding a place, and the partner's members are emailed; a keeper grants or declines it once, from the version they read, and the person who asked is emailed; a declined request gives its place back and its team does not ask again; the partner downloads the claims with a column for each question, and the download is recorded | A request with missing or foreign answers, a last place given twice, a decision on another partner's request or on one already decided, a decline that keeps its place, or claimants' email taken out with no record | `PerkTest.aRequestWaitsForThePartnerWhoseAnswerIsEmailedAndADeclineGivesThePlaceBack` |
| A request without approval is granted as it is sent and nobody is asked; a perk got once per person gives each member a claim of their own, and one claim to each | Every request held for an answer nobody gives, or one claim shared by a team for a personal perk | `PerkTest.aRequestWithoutApprovalIsGrantedAsItIsSentAndOncePerPersonEachMemberGetsTheirOwn` |
| A perk shows from its start; one that ended leaves the catalogue unless the ended ones are asked for, and its page says it ended; neither is got before its start or after its end; a perk ends after it starts | A perk got outside its dates, an ended perk crowding the catalogue, or dates in the wrong order | `PerkTest.aPerkShowsBetweenItsDatesAndAnEndedOneIsListedOnlyWhenAsked` |
| A perk for a program's applicants or shortlist names a program that exists, and an operator adds a perk only to programs that exist | A program's perk that no team can ever get | `PerkTest.aPerkForAProgramsTeamsNamesAProgramThatExists` |
| A program's page lists the perks for its teams to anyone; a team that applied gets the applicants' perk and one that did not is told it is for applicants; the shortlist's perk opens to a shortlisted team only once the outcomes are released, and a team not selected is told it is for the shortlist | A shortlist leaked by a perk before the release, or an applicants' perk taken by a team that never applied | `ReviewTest.aPerkForTheShortlistOpensToTheTeamsShortlistedOnceTheOutcomesAreReleased` |
| `perk` is a closed module that depends on `audit`, `identity`, `notification`, `organization`, `program`, `proposal`, `solution` and `shared` only, and `search` and `mcp` read it through its published API | A dependency cycle through `proposal`, or a module reaching into the perk's tables | `ModulithArchitectureTest` |
| The three perk emails render in their default wording with their links as buttons | A template naming a variable its kind does not declare | `EmailRendererTest` |
