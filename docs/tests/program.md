# Program: verification matrix

Boundaries follow [conventions › Testing](../conventions.md#testing). An operator's change is an HTTP request, a row, its lists and an audit event, and a visitor's read depends on the session and the clock, so the program tests start the full application against PostgreSQL and speak real HTTP. Sign-in goes through the real code flow with the SMTP server replaced. Where a program stands is a function of its dates and a moment, so its boundaries are a unit test.

## The admin screens

`web/tests/e2e/admin-programs.spec.ts` runs on desktop and mobile Chrome with axe; the programs a page reads on the web server are answered by `tests/e2e/stub-backend.mjs`, and the commands the browser sends by the test, which checks the CSRF header on each. The browser runs in Paris, so a time written or sent in another zone than Vietnam's shows. It covers: nobody but an operator gets the list or a program's Settings; the list shows each program's state and window, in Vietnam time; New program suggests the address from the name, Vietnamese included, sends the three members and opens the draft's Settings; a taken address is told beside the field; a draft lists what blocks publishing, each line saying Missing or Done and focusing its field, and Publish becomes Save and publish once something changed; a save sends the whole program with its window and key dates as instants of Vietnam time; a date of the window stands in the list under the words the page shows, is renamed in a dialog, and the title is sent with the window; dates out of order are refused before anything is sent; someone else's save in the meantime is told above the form; a published program's address is locked, its phase and what it waits for next stand beside its badge, when its applications open and the removal of its cover are closed with the reason once they would be refused, and a key date leads with its date; unpublishing and Save and publish each ask first, show the address, and send one command; Feature on home page in the menu sends one command and its toast's Undo stops featuring; an operator adds questions in a dialog, one answered by one choice and one by several, moves one with the focus following it and the move said, is asked before leaving with them unsaved, and saves them together, and the questions of a program whose applications are open are shown as fixed.

Unit tests beside the code cover the address made from a name, and an event's rules: its city when it is held in person and an end that has both its day and its time (`program-schemas.test.ts`), the program as Settings holds it and as it is sent back (`program-values.test.ts`), the days and times of Vietnam (`lib/vietnam-time.test.ts`) and the field a validation problem points at (`lib/api/problem-fields.test.ts`).

Checked by hand on the local stack on 5 October: a cover uploaded to the local store and shown in Settings, a program saved, a refused save, publishing a new program after filling what blocked it, and unpublishing it.

## The public pages

`web/tests/e2e/programs.spec.ts` runs on desktop and mobile Chrome with axe, the programs answered by `tests/e2e/stub-backend.mjs` with dates counted from the moment it starts. It covers: what is open and what is coming up are one list, each card labelled program or event (BEY-199): a program open now with its countdown and Apply, a program not started, an event and nothing else with its cover, place and Register, and a program's own events on its card and not as cards of their own; what is done; never a draft; a program whose page is somewhere else links there; the phase tabs and the type are the address; a standard page shows the program, its About and its timeline, and no use case section when none is attached; the Tasco page is the one made for it, its timeline taken from the program, the use cases attached to it listed and named in its navigation, the sections carried over from the campaign's earlier page (the client, the notes beside the timeline, the six steps of the pilot month with what to bring and when each is done, the design requirements, the support, the five myths and the thirteen questions) each present and reached from a navigation that wraps within the screen, its card to apply before the challenge on a phone, and its Vietnamese copy at `/vi`; the Nestlé page is the one made for it, its deadline (above the page and among the four facts), its timeline and its event taken from the program, each section reached from the navigation, the six challenge areas as tabs over one panel that change what it shows, the use cases attached to it, the four steps, what an applicant needs and the six questions, the supporting activities in their three groups, the eight questions, the closing call and the note about the programme, nothing wider than the screen, its card to apply before the first section on a phone, and its Vietnamese copy at `/vi`; a draft answers 404 to a visitor and shows an operator a banner that leads back to Settings; an unknown address is not found.
