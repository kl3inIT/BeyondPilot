# Organization: verification matrix

Boundaries follow [conventions › Testing](../conventions.md#testing). Membership is rows in four tables read on every request, so `OrganizationTest` starts the full application against PostgreSQL and speaks real HTTP, signing in through the emailed code as the identity tests do.

`OrganizationTest` covers the contracts below; the screens are covered by `web/tests/e2e/workspace-organization.spec.ts` and `web/tests/e2e/admin-organizations.spec.ts`, on desktop and mobile Chrome with axe, the reads answered by `tests/e2e/stub-backend.mjs` and the commands by the test.

| Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Regression it catches                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A person creates an organization, becomes its owner, and it waits for review                                                                                                                                                               | An organization visible before GenAI Fund saw it; a creator who is not its owner                      |
| A new organization carries its industries, and its creator joins as owner                                                                                                                                              | Industries dropped on the way in; an owner who is not a member                                |
| A creation without a country, a team size, or an industry answers `400` and points at each                                                                                                                                     | An organization in review that lacks what the review reads                                            |
| A creation and a save require the website, a description of at most 280 characters and a founded year from 1800 to 2100, and keep the logo; each is refused by its pointer                                                         | A profile the review cannot read; a description too long for a card; a logo that is not a stored image |
| A logo is an uploaded `organization_logo` image that only one organization names; naming a foreign or unknown file answers `400 ORGANIZATION_LOGO_NOT_USABLE`, and replacing or clearing it deletes the former file | A logo shared between organizations; a stored file nobody names |
| The public page of an approved organization carries its logo, and null where it has none | A public page that draws initials for an organization with a logo |
| An invitation lapses seven days after it is sent; a lapsed one is not listed, not answered (`404 ORGANIZATION_INVITATION_NOT_FOUND`), frees its place in the open limit, and its address may be invited again | An invitation that stays open for ever; a lapsed invitation that blocks a new one to the same address |
| The members are read ten at a time, owners first, with the total; an owner reads every open invitation on any page; a page below 1 answers `400`; the web shows the invitations on the last page only                                      | A page that repeats or drops a person; invitations cut off by the paging; a count read from one page  |
| A person belongs to one organization: creating, joining or accepting a second one is refused                                                                                                                                               | Two memberships for one account                                                                       |
| An address on the domain an operator verified joins at once when an owner let it; otherwise it asks, as any other address does                                                                                                             | Anyone joining any organization; a domain nobody checked letting people in                            |
| A creator and an invited first owner give the organization no domain; only an operator's approval or claim decision sets one, and a domain another organization has answers `409`                                                          | A domain taken from an address nobody verified; two organizations on one domain                       |
| Joining at once is refused for an organization that is not approved or has no verified domain                                                                                                                                              | Auto-join on a domain nobody checked                                                                  |
| Only an approved organization invites; the 21st invitation in 24 hours and the 51st open one answer `429` by their codes, and a revoked one still counts for the day                                                                       | An organization used to send mail to strangers; a limit reset by revoking                             |
| An owner reads how many invitations are left today and how many may still be open                                                                                                                                                          | A form offered that cannot be sent                                                                    |
| A declined request is read back by the person until they ask again or get in; the decision is emailed                                                                                                                                      | A person left without an answer                                                                       |
| An owner invites by email; only that address sees, accepts or declines the invitation; an owner can withdraw it                                                                                                                            | An invitation accepted by another account; an invitation that cannot be undone                        |
| An owner approves or declines a request to join; a member cannot                                                                                                                                                                           | A member admitting people                                                                             |
| An owner changes a role and removes a member; the last owner cannot leave or be demoted                                                                                                                                                    | An organization without an owner                                                                      |
| A stale `version` on save answers `409`                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Two owners overwriting each other's profile                                                           |
| An operator approves, or refuses with a reason; saving a refused organization sends it for review again                                                                                                                                    | A refusal the owners cannot answer; a refused organization that stays refused                         |
| A claim on an organization nobody owns always waits for an operator, whatever the claimant's address; approval makes them its owner, the open claims of others become requests to join, and the decision is emailed                        | Anyone taking over an unowned organization by its domain; a claim nobody answers                      |
| The operators' list says what each organization asks, who asked and when; the queue holds new organizations and open claims                                                                                                                | A claim on an approved organization that no queue shows                                               |
| An operator adds an organization that is approved at once, needing only its name and type, with any other part of the profile, the email domain and an owner invited or none                                               | A company GenAI Fund lists that must wait for its own review                                                            |
| Every operator decision and every change of role or removal is in the audit log                                                                                                                                                            | A decision nobody can trace                                                                           |
| The admin paths answer `403` to a user and `401` without a session                                                                                                                                                                         | The review screens open to everyone                                                                   |
| Anyone reads the page of an approved organization; one that waits or was refused answers not found                                                                                                                                         | A company page behind sign-in; an unreviewed organization shown to the public                         |
| An operator takes an approved organization down with a reason from a closed list and a note, and restores it; only an approved one can be taken down, only a taken-down one restored                                       | A take-down of a pending organization, or a restore of an approved one, being accepted                                  |
| A taken-down organization keeps its members and their workspace, which reads the reason; its page answers not found, its solutions leave the directories, and its people cannot invite or let a domain join                | A suspended organization still listed publicly or still inviting                                                        |
| The owners are emailed when their organization is taken down and when it is restored; both are in the audit log                                                                                                            | A take-down or restore nobody was told about or that left no record                                                     |
| An operator saves the profile and the verified domain of any organization with the version check; a domain another organization has answers `409`; clearing the domain turns joining at once off                           | An operator overwriting a newer save, or two organizations sharing one domain                                           |
| An operator changes a role, removes a member, invites an address and withdraws an invitation of any organization, and may leave it without an owner; their invitations stay out of its limits; each is in the audit log    | An operator blocked by the last-owner rule, or an operator's invitation using up the organization's allowance           |
| The operator's record shows the profile and the members in two tabs, the members ten to a page with the open invitations on the last, the take-down and restore dialogs, and a role change that leaves no owner asks first | A record that drops the invitations, pages them with the people, or demotes the only owner without telling the operator |
| An owner's save publishes `OrganizationChanged`, after which search shows the organization's solutions under its new name (`SearchDirectoriesTest.anApprovedSolutionIsFoundUnderItsOrganizationAndLeavesTheResultsWhenUnlistedOrRejected`) | A solution found under a name its organization no longer has                                          |
