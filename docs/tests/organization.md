# Organization: verification matrix

BEY-133's required-only popup collects 2/4/7 facts for Just me/A team/A company, with short description including team experience and no separate organization-background input. `RequiredOrganizationBasicsTest` checks conditional description, company facts and limits. The HTTP cases in `OrganizationTest` check creation, trimming, optional-fact and legacy-data preservation, authority and stale versions. `organization-schemas.test.ts` covers active-only validation and serialization; `organization-form.test.tsx` checks removal of the old input. `proposals.spec.ts` covers all three create payloads, field visibility, switching, versioned editing and rereading on desktop/mobile. Editing keeps the main branch's individual-type restrictions; solo records are approved at creation.

Boundaries follow [conventions › Testing](../conventions.md#testing). Membership is rows in four tables read on every request, so `OrganizationTest` starts the full application against PostgreSQL and speaks real HTTP, signing in through the emailed code as the identity tests do.

`OrganizationTest` covers the contracts below; the screens are covered by `web/tests/e2e/workspace-organization.spec.ts` and `web/tests/e2e/admin-organizations.spec.ts`, on desktop and mobile Chrome with axe, the reads answered by `tests/e2e/stub-backend.mjs` and the commands by the test. A Just me is read as any organization there (the `alone` account: menu, tabs, profile) and in `proposals.spec.ts` (Edit and My organization in the organization card and the Review step); `organization-form.test.tsx` covers the switch from Just me to a team and back, and `organization-frame.test.tsx` its tabs.
