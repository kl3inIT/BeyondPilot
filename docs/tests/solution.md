# Solution: verification matrix

Boundaries follow [conventions › Testing](../conventions.md#testing). Who may write a solution is read from the caller's organization on every request, so `SolutionTest` starts the full application against PostgreSQL and speaks real HTTP, signing in through the emailed code as the identity tests do.

| Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Regression it catches                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Every member of an organization creates, saves, submits and deletes, approved or not; someone with no organization is refused with `SOLUTION_MEMBER_REQUIRED`                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A person with no organization publishing; a member kept from the work                                                                                                        |
| A draft needs only a name; a submission needs a summary, a maturity, a focus area, an industry, a logo and a cover, and the list of an organization names what each solution lacks. A save of a submitted or approved solution takes away nothing a review needs, and asks for nothing the solution never had                                                              | An empty entry in the directory                                                                                                                                              |
| A stale `version` on save answers `409`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Two owners overwriting each other                                                                                                                                            |
| Only a draft is deleted                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | A reviewed solution vanishing with its history                                                                                                                               |
| An operator decides only a solution `in_review`: approves it, sends it back with what to change (`needs_changes`, 1 to 1000 characters), which its owners correct and send again, which clears the send back, or refuses it for good with a reason other than missing information (`rejected`), which its owners can neither save nor send again | A rejection used as a send back; a refused solution sent again; a decision made twice |
| The members of the organization are emailed when its solution is approved, sent back with what to change, refused, taken down or restored | An owner never told what GenAI Fund decided |
| An operator takes an approved solution down with a reason from a closed list and a note, and restores it without a new review once its organization is approved and not taken down; its review stays `approved` while `suspendedAt` says it is down, its owners read why and cannot send it for review, and the operators' list filters it as `suspended` and leaves it out of `approved` | A listing GenAI Fund cannot remove; a takedown that loses the approval; a takedown that the owners undo themselves |
| The directory shows only approved and listed solutions that are not taken down, filtered by search, industry, focus area and maturity                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | A draft, a rejected or a hidden solution shown to the public                                                                                                                 |
| The public paths need no session; everything else does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The directory behind sign-in; an editor open to visitors                                                                                                                     |
| Each approval, send back, rejection, takedown and restore is in the audit log | A decision nobody can trace |
| The response to a submission carries the version the next save sends                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | A save right after a submission refused as stale                                                                                                                             |
| The directory lists by name, or the most recently approved solutions first when asked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | An order the visitor chose and did not get                                                                                                                                   |
| The directory narrows to the solutions of one organization, by the address of its public page                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Another organization's solutions on a company's page                                                                                                                         |
| A customer deployment is read by its organization and by operators at once, and by the public only after an operator approves it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | An unreviewed claim about a customer on a public page                                                                                                                        |
| A change to an approved customer deployment takes it off the public pages until it is approved again; a save from a stale form is refused                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | An edited claim shown without review; a silent overwrite                                                                                                                     |
| Only an operator decides on a customer deployment, each decision is recorded in the audit trail, and only the owners of its solution change or remove it                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A provider approving its own claim; another organization editing it                                                                                                          |
| An approved solution left unlisted is absent from the directory, opens by its address and says it is not listed; a draft, one in review or sent back, a rejected one and one taken down still answer `404`                                                                                                                                                                                                | An unlisted solution answering `404`, or a solution that is not approved read by its address                                                                                 |
| The demo is an optional `http(s)` link written by the owners and shown on the public page; another scheme is refused                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | A required field blocking an early-stage provider; a `javascript:` address on a public page                                                                                  |
| Traction, what it is built with, languages and the best customer profile are kept trimmed and without repeats, shown on the public page, and none is needed for a review; an unknown language and an empty name are refused                                                                                                                                                | A new field that blocks a submission; a code the directory cannot name                                                                                                       |
| A deck is a stored PDF of purpose `solution_deck` that the caller uploaded and no other solution names; a replaced or removed deck, and the deck of a deleted draft, leave the store                                                                                                                                                                                       | Another organization's file, or a file of another purpose, published as a deck; files nobody names piling up                                                                 |
| A logo is a stored image of purpose `solution_logo`, a cover and up to four images under it of purpose `solution_image`: each uploaded by the caller, or already the solution's, named once and by one solution, kept in the order given. An image a solution stops naming, and those of a deleted draft, leave the store. Anyone reads the images of an approved solution | Another organization's image, or a file of another kind, shown on a public page; one file shared by two solutions and removed with one of them; files nobody names piling up |
| What GenAI Fund says of a solution, who backs its company, its programme and its funding, is written only by an operator, on a solution that was sent for review; it is recorded in the audit trail, shown on the card and the page, left alone by a save of the owners, and gone when emptied                                                                             | An owner naming their own solution as backed by GenAI Fund; a save by the owners erasing what an operator wrote                                                              |
| A deck is read by anyone once its solution is approved; before that, and after it is taken down, by the members of its organization and by the operators once it was sent to them                                                                                                                                                                                          | An unreviewed deck read by its address; a draft's existence revealed by its deck                                                                                             |
| A request for an introduction is accepted for an approved solution, listed or not                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The way to reach a provider closed by a visibility choice                                                                                                                    |
| The operators' list matches a solution by its name or by its organization's name, narrows by industry, names who sent each one, and counts what waits whatever narrows it                                                                                                                                                                                                  | A search field that promises the organization and matches the solution only; a queue that hides how much waits                                                               |
| The operators' list is read 25 at a time, the longest wait first; a page past the last is empty and a page before the first is refused                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | A queue that drops or repeats a solution between pages                                                                                                                       |
| A solution sent before the sender was recorded names nobody, to operators and to its owners, and names the sender once it is sent again                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | A guess shown as the sender; a failure on the rows that existed before the column                                                                                            |
| A save and every decision publish `SolutionChanged`, after which search finds an approved solution under its organization, keeps an unlisted one out of a visitor's results, and drops one taken down from the index, matching included, until it is restored (`SearchDirectoriesTest.anApprovedSolutionIsFoundUnderItsOrganizationAndLeavesTheResultsWhenUnlistedOrTakenDown`) | A solution found by search or matched after it was taken down or unlisted |
