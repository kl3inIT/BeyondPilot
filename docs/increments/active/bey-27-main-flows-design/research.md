# BEY-27 — Research behind the main flows

What was studied on 3 October 2026 before the screens were drawn, what was taken from each reference, and where the real content on the screens comes from. Links are to public pages; no screenshot of a third party is kept in the repository.

## Campaign pages

A program page persuades before it informs, so the references are real campaigns, not form patterns.

| Reference                                                                                                             | What it does well                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Taken into the design                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Agentic AI Build Week](https://aabw.genaifund.ai/) (GenAI Fund)                                                      | An identity of its own; a hero with the name, a line of proof numbers, three facts and two actions; partner logos in tiers; "This isn't another hackathon" differentiators; tracks as numbered rows with the owner's logo; after the event, a scale band and winners per track                                                      | The cover hero with three proof numbers; numbered directions; the "what winning gets you" section; the ended state with scale numbers and winners per track                              |
| [OpenAI Build Week](https://openai.com/build-week/) and its [Devpost page](https://openai.devpost.com/)               | One summary card with dates, format, prize and participant count; section tabs (overview, rules, resources, FAQ); "How to participate" as numbered steps; "What to build" and "What to submit" stated separately; judging criteria as questions; after the event, the page turns into a winners gallery by category with the judges | The section navigation under the cover; the Apply rail as the one summary card; "What to submit" with numbered items and the judging criteria; the page's lifecycle from open to results |
| [OpenAI Codex Hackathon](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/openai-codex-hackathon) (Cerebral Valley)                        | A poster cover beside the facts; hosts, date and place above the fold; one action that changes with the event's state ("Event finished")                                                                                                                                                                                            | The action that follows the program's state: Apply while open, the recap and winners once ended                                                                                          |
| [AI for Insurance Challenge × Tasco](https://beyondpilot.genaifund.ai/insurance-ai-tasco) (the interim campaign page) | The content itself: the question, the one constraint, eight directions in three stages, six dates, "No cash prize. A buyer instead.", the submission list, judges and FAQ                                                                                                                                                           | All copy on the open program page and the fields of the application form                                                                                                                 |

openai.com refuses headless browsers; its structure was read through the Devpost mirror and a text rendering. aabw.genaifund.ai renders in the browser, so its text was read after load.

## What the research changed

- The first draft of the program page was a document with an Apply rail. It now opens with a cover, proof numbers and a section navigation, and it has a second life after the event.
- "What winning gets you" was missing from the first draft although it is the campaign's main argument.
- The application form's fields come from the campaign's own submission list, not from a generic "proposal" form.
- The directory's use case cards became text-first: the photographs on hand show events, not the use cases.
