# BEY-245: People register for GenAI Fund's Luma events on BeyondPilot

Status: proposed by Đạt and Claude on 23 October 2026, for Đạt's approval ([plan](plan.md)). Tracked in Linear as [BEY-245](https://linear.app/beyondpilot/issue/BEY-245). It extends [BEY-190](../bey-190-luma-events/design.md). What was read before this design is in [Registering for a Luma event on our own site](../../../research/2026-10-23-luma-registration.md); the flow is drawn in `.tmp/luma-registration-flow.html`.

## Why

Kai Yong, 23 October 2026: "We want the user to register without leaving the page. The luma api can supply the registration questions and settings. Ideally user experience should completely stay on beyond pilot. We also want users to be notified and the site updated for them when they go from registered -> rejected / approved." Then: "whoever wants to sign up for the event will be onboarded as a user on our platform as well, experience wise it should be seamless". Eugene, for the page: "First version, just mimic Luma first."

GenAI Fund sent a product brief and an API note the same day, has Luma Plus, and made `team@beyondpilot.ai` an admin of the calendar "GenAI Fund - Official" (`cal-KxQE75V2Ywj0ZDI`).

Today an event is a program of type `event` read every hour from the calendar's public iCal feed and each event page's JSON-LD (BEY-190). Its page is `external`: Register opens Luma, BeyondPilot never learns who registered, and the person does not become a user.

## Domain story

1. An operator pastes the calendar's API key on Admin › Programs › **Luma**. BeyondPilot checks it, finds the calendar, makes its webhook and reads the events.
2. Kai publishes "TinyFish, Big Minds" on Luma, with approval required and four questions. Within a minute BeyondPilot has the program, its cover, its time with its time zone, its city, and the venue kept for confirmed guests.
3. Linh, not signed in, opens the event on BeyondPilot. The page reads like Luma's: the cover and hosts on the left; the name, the date and place, a registration card and the description on the right.
4. She chooses **Request to join**, types her name, her email and the answers, and chooses **Continue**. The card asks for the 6-digit code sent to her address; she types it. She now has an account, is signed in, and agrees to the platform's use of her data in the same card.
5. BeyondPilot keeps her registration, then adds her to the Luma event as pending approval with Luma's emails off, and emails her that the request was received. The card says it waits for review.
6. GenAI Fund approves her on Luma, as today. Luma tells BeyondPilot; BeyondPilot reads her guest record, the card says **You're in** and shows the venue, and she gets one email with Luma's check-in QR and a calendar file.
7. An operator opens the event on Admin › Programs › **Registrations**: who registered here, the status on Luma, and whether the send to Luma worked.

Failure paths:

- Luma refuses or does not answer the add: the registration stays, marked not sent; a job tries again and an operator can retry. The person is never told she is on Luma before she is.
- Luma answers 200 but lists her email as skipped (she unsubscribed from the calendar or was removed): the registration is marked failed with that reason for an operator.
- A host edits the questions after Linh opened the form: the questions are read again before the send, and a form that no longer matches asks her to look again.
- A webhook is lost or arrives twice or out of order: a delivery is deduplicated and only makes BeyondPilot read the guest again; an hourly check reads the guest lists of events still to come.
- The key is removed or rejected: nothing is read or sent; Admin says so; registrations wait.

## Decisions

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An event stays a program of type `event`; a registration belongs to `program`, in `program.luma`, which gains a dependency on `notification` (an ADR when the work starts)                                                                                     | The list, cards, search and home page already show such a program (BEY-190). A registration is a guest of a Luma event, not an application judged by reviewers, which `proposal` owns. `notification` depends on no module that depends on `program`, so the edge makes no cycle |
| The calendar is read through Luma's API (`calendars/events/list`, `events/get`) and webhooks (`event.created`, `event.updated`, `event.canceled`, `calendar.event.added`) instead of the iCal feed and the JSON-LD, which go                                   | Only the API gives the questions, the time zone, whether registration is open and needs approval, and lets BeyondPilot add a guest. BEY-190 planned this switch once GenAI Fund had Luma Plus. One source, not two modes                                                         |
| The API key and the webhook's secret are configuration an operator keeps in Admin, sealed with AES-256-GCM under `BEYONDPILOT_PROGRAM_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, never returned                                                                                          | ADR 0005 and 0006 keep provider secrets this way. The key never travels through chat or a host file                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Saving a key checks it (`users/get-self`), finds its calendar, then makes the webhook (`v2/webhooks/create`) after listing the existing ones, pointing at this environment's public address                                                                    | Nobody registers a webhook by hand or sends an address around (codex-events, nashville-takeover)                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| An event GenAI Fund manages registers on BeyondPilot. One that another host only lists on the calendar, one that asks a `terms` question, and a paid one keep their Luma link                                                                                  | The key reads the questions and adds guests only for managed events. Luma's add has no answer for `terms`. Paying needs Luma's checkout                                                                                                                                          |
| The page imitates Luma's event page: cover and hosts left; name, date with time zone, place, the registration card and the description right; on a phone the card follows the date and the hosts go last                                                       | Eugene asked for it. The venue Luma keeps for guests is shown only to an approved person                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| The form is built from the event's `registration_questions`: text (one line or several), select (one or several of its options), agree-check, company with job title, phone, URL and the social handles. They are read again before a registration is accepted | A question written in code fails the whole add when the host edits the form (Habinext)                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| A person not signed in types the emailed code in the card, using the sign-in by code that exists; a new account agrees to the platform's use of data in the card, recorded with source `application` as the apply flow does                                    | Đạt chose this on 23 October: the person becomes a user at once, the email is known to be theirs, and the status is there when they come back. Luma itself asks no code; this is the one step more                                                                               |
| A registration is stored before Luma is called, with the answers and the questions as asked, and a send state: waiting, sent, failed with a reason. A job sends after the commit and retries with backoff; an operator retries a failed one                    | Zectix and codex-events keep a send state; the person's request is never lost to Luma being down                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| The add is `pending_approval` when the event requires approval and `approved` otherwise, always with `send_email: false`. The guest is then found by email (`guests/get`) and kept by its `gst-` id                                                            | The add returns no id. Luma's default, approved with its own email, would skip review and send a second email                                                                                                                                                                    |
| A webhook delivery is verified (HMAC-SHA256 of `t.body` with the secret, ±5 minutes), deduplicated by `Webhook-Id`, answered 2xx at once, and only triggers a read of the guest or the event from Luma                                                         | A payload carries no order (hack0, nashville-takeover). The `guest.registered` that our own add causes finds the registration already there (Habinext)                                                                                                                           |
| BeyondPilot sends three emails: request received, you're in (with Luma's `check_in_qr_code` and an .ics), and declined; each once per real change of status, recorded on the registration. Luma's reminders before the event still go                          | GenAI Fund's brief. The QR and the calendar file keep what Luma's own email gave. Operators reword the kinds in Admin › Email as any other                                                                                                                                       |
| An hour after the last check, the guest lists of events still to come are read again (`guests/list`)                                                                                                                                                           | Catches a webhook missed or a decision made with Luma's notification left on                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Registrations are kept as applications are, with no retention period                                                                                                                                                                                           | Decided by Đạt on 23 October; no other personal data in BeyondPilot has one                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Admin: a **Luma** tab on Programs (key, calendar, webhook, last read, Pull from Luma), and a **Registrations** tab on an event's program (person, answers, status on Luma, send state, Retry, export)                                                          | Approving stays on Luma, where GenAI Fund works today                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

## What changes

- **Backend, `program`.** Migrations for the Luma connection (sealed key and secret, calendar, webhook id, last read and error), the program's new event fields (time zone, venue and its visibility, registration open, approval required, registration through BeyondPilot or Luma), the registrations, and the webhook deliveries. `program.luma`: the API client replacing `LumaFeed` and `LumaEventPage`, the webhook endpoint, the send job, the reconciliation, the email kinds through `EmailService`. `ProgramAdministration` gains the connection and the registrations; `ProgramService` the public event with its form and the caller's registration. ical4j and, if nothing else uses it, jsoup leave.
- **Backend, `config`.** The webhook path is public and outside the CSRF check; its signature is its authentication.
- **Backend, `notification`.** Three kinds with default wording, in the Events group.
- **Web.** The event page and its registration card with the code step; Admin's Luma and Registrations tabs; the account's events. `openapi.yml` and the generated client.
- **Deployment.** `program-encryption-key` in each environment's secrets, required by `deploy.sh`.
- **Documents.** The ADR, `docs/specs/program.md`, `docs/specs/notification.md`, `docs/tests/program.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, the runbooks.

## Not done

- Paid events, several tickets, group registration, a waitlist offer accepted on BeyondPilot.
- A person cancelling or changing their answers on BeyondPilot: Luma's API offers neither to a guest; they reply to the email.
- Approving on BeyondPilot: GenAI Fund keeps doing it on Luma.
- Luma's page sending people to BeyondPilot (the brief's phase 3): a setting GenAI Fund changes on Luma.

## Known limits

- A guest already on the Luma event keeps their earlier answers there; BeyondPilot keeps both.
- An operator who approves on Luma with its notification on sends the person Luma's email as well as ours.
- Between the release and the key being pasted on an environment, its events are not read.
