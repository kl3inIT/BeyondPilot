# BeyondPilot roadmap

## Delivered

- [BEY-96 — Operators download a list as a spreadsheet](increments/completed/bey-96-spreadsheet-exports/design.md): the Export menu of Admin's lists (applications, drafts, a use case's proposals, organizations, solutions, talent, AI calls, audit log) downloads the list as the screen narrows it, every page of it, as an Excel workbook styled for reading or as CSV, through one `Spreadsheet` in a new `shared` module ([ADR 0009](decisions/0009-a-shared-module-writes-the-spreadsheets-operators-download.md)). Operators only, each download recorded. Built and checked on staging on 14 October 2026: every list downloaded in both formats and recorded in the audit log; report templates wait in BEY-194.
- [BEY-189 — Someone applying on their own is themselves](increments/completed/bey-189-solo-applicant/design.md), 14 October 2026: choosing Individual makes an organization that is approved at once; its rule that the organization is hidden, uneditable and fixed in its type was reversed by this increment.
- [A Just me is an ordinary organization](increments/completed/just-me-is-an-organization/design.md), 14 October 2026: an organization of type Just me is visible and editable like any other, and switches between Just me, a team, a company and other; it is still approved at once and has one member, no invitations and no domain.
- [BEY-176 — Matching: one solution at a time](increments/completed/bey-176-matching-review/design.md), 14 October 2026: the matched solutions read one card at a time beside the deck, with Save, Not a fit, Later, a drag on the card's head, keys and Undo; a removal needs no reason.
- [BEY-39 — Matching](increments/completed/bey-39-matching/design.md), 14 October 2026: for a use case, the solutions on BeyondPilot that fit, each with what its own material shows per requirement and the sentence that shows it, in GenAI Fund's three groups; people shortlist, remove and add candidates, and say whether a group is right (BEY-106).
- [BEY-92 — AI models](increments/completed/bey-92-ai-models/design.md), 14 October 2026: the `ai` module where operators connect chat providers, choose the model each task uses with its reasoning level, and every call is recorded with its tokens.
- [BEY-78 — MCP](increments/completed/bey-78-mcp/design.md), 14 October 2026: the user and operator MCP servers behind OAuth, Admin › AI › MCP with its tools, connected apps and activity, and the account's MCP page. A live connection from each AI client is still to be checked.
- [BEY-155 — Nestlé Vietnam AI Reinvention](increments/completed/bey-155-nestle-program-page/design.md), 14 October 2026: the program's own page and application form on BeyondPilot, moved from its Lovable page.
- [BEY-130 — Featured directory items](increments/completed/bey-130-featured-directory-items/design.md), 14 October 2026: an operator features solutions, talent profiles and use cases, which come first in the opening view of their lists; Admin's lists mark them Featured.
- [BEY-128 — Brevo as an email provider](increments/completed/bey-128-brevo-email/design.md), 14 October 2026: a fourth provider beside SES, Resend and SMTP, with delivery reports and the domain checklist. Production sends through Resend; a first send with a real Brevo account is still to be made.
- [BEY-129 — Several choices to a question](increments/completed/bey-129-multi-choice-question/design.md), 14 October 2026: a program question answered by several of its options, so the Tasco form asks what the Lovable form asked.
- [BEY-90 — Import of the Agentic AI Build Week registrations](increments/completed/bey-90-aabw-talent-import/design.md), 11 October 2026: the approved builders who stated their work became talent profiles, each with an account its owner signs in to; all 2,117 are listed on production after GenAI Fund's go-ahead.
- [BEY-74 — Import from the old platform](increments/completed/bey-74-v1-import/design.md), 11 October 2026: the startups, use cases, people as invitations and enterprise profiles of app.genaifund.ai, loaded on staging and production, with the duplicates merged (BEY-75, BEY-147, BEY-156).
- [BEY-104 — AI usage](increments/completed/bey-104-ai-usage/design.md), 10 October 2026: every call to an AI provider in a log, with what succeeded, what failed and what it cost, on a screen of Admin › AI.
- [BEY-103 — A price follows the catalog](increments/completed/bey-103-prices-follow-the-catalog/design.md), 10 October 2026: a model's or an OCR provider's price is the bundled catalog's unless an operator sets one.
- [BEY-102 — Document reading with OCR providers](increments/completed/bey-102-document-reading-ocr/design.md), 9 October 2026: operators connect an OCR service and choose it to read the pages of a PDF that are only pictures, in a tab of its own in Admin › AI › Providers.
- [BEY-76 — One review lifecycle](increments/completed/bey-76-review-lifecycle/design.md), 8 October 2026: organizations, solutions, use cases and talent profiles share one set of review statuses and words.
- [BEY-32 — Storage](increments/completed/bey-32-storage/design.md), 6 October 2026: files kept and served through an adapter, the local disk now and S3 later, with the web upload helper.
- [BEY-27 — Main flows as UI design](increments/completed/bey-27-main-flows-design/design.md), 6 October 2026: the program pages, the application flow and the directory drawn in Figma for the 6 October milestone.
- [BEY-175 — Admin home](increments/completed/bey-175-admin-home/design.md), 13 October 2026: every review queue with how long its oldest has waited and its last 14 days, the programs with applications to read and how many the operator assessed, and the AI calls, email and search index of the last 7 days; built on the Motion and Charts conventions (BEY-174)
- [BEY-33 — Organizations, the solution catalog and talent profiles](increments/completed/bey-33-organization-solution-talent/design.md), 7 October 2026: organizations and membership with their review, take-down and merge by operators ([the flows completed and what operators manage](increments/completed/bey-33-organization-flows-and-admin/design.md), BEY-61 to BEY-63), solutions and talent profiles with their review, and the public directories.
- [BEY-68 — Email that operators run](increments/completed/bey-68-email-delivery/design.md), 6 October 2026: email through Amazon SES, Resend or SMTP, chosen and set up by operators in Admin › Email with their secrets sealed; twenty-four kinds of email worded as English templates operators can edit with a live preview; a queue and log with retries, delivery reports and suppressed addresses; a setup checklist that asks the provider about the domain and its DNS records. Staging sends from `beyondpilot.vadan.app`; production waits on the DNS of `beyondpilot.ai`.
- [BEY-72 — AI providers and the search index in Admin](increments/completed/bey-72-ai-providers/design.md), 6 October 2026: operators connect the embedding provider and choose its model in Admin › AI, its key sealed in the database, and follow the search index: what is embedded, what the provider refused, and semantic search on or off.
- [BEY-65 — Search](increments/completed/bey-65-search/design.md), 6 October 2026: one index over programs, use cases, AI solutions and AI talent behind `/search`, ranked by words and, with embeddings, by meaning (BEY-69, BEY-70); a tab per kind, each with its count.
- [BEY-38 — Reviewing applications and releasing outcomes](increments/completed/bey-38-review/design.md), 6 October 2026: a program's judging criteria, judges invited by email who score the applications, GenAI Fund's decisions kept private until it releases them, and every applicant told on the same day.
- [BEY-37 — Applying to a program](increments/completed/bey-37-proposal/design.md), 6 October 2026: sign in and return to the form, a four-step application saved as a draft, submission with its receipt, My applications, and changes or withdrawal while the window is open.
- [BEY-29 — Programs](increments/completed/bey-29-program/design.md), 6 October 2026: operators create, edit, publish and unpublish programs with their window, key dates, events and cover, each change audited; the public list by phase and type, the standard program page and the page made for the AI for Insurance Challenge. The 2026 programs are entered on production.
- [BEY-49 — Admin: the audit log](increments/completed/bey-49-admin-audit-log/design.md), 5 October 2026: the operators' reading of the audit record at `/admin/audit-log`, narrowed by period, action and a search, and walked by cursor.
- [BEY-30 — Identity](increments/completed/bey-30-identity/design.md), 4 October 2026: sign-in with Google and with an emailed six-digit code that returns a person where they were, sessions, the operator role, and the signed-in header.
- [BEY-48 — Admin: accounts](increments/completed/bey-48-admin-accounts/design.md), 4 October 2026: the operators' list of everyone who has signed in, granting and withdrawing the operator role, disabling and enabling an account, and the audit record of each change.

## Active

- [BEY-105 — Invite a recommended provider to propose](increments/active/bey-105-invite-to-propose/design.md): members and operators invite a matched provider from the card, the panel or the row menu, and its owners get an email with a link to the use case. On production since 14 October 2026, with Proposal received once the provider has sent one (#320); the first real invitation, sent by a person of GenAI Fund's choosing, is still to come.
- [BEY-173 — Messages](increments/active/bey-173-messaging/design.md): one live chat for the platform in place of the talent enquiries, which ended in an exchange of email addresses. A person writes to a solution's provider, to the person behind a talent profile or to GenAI Fund about a program; a first message to someone new waits under Requests until it is accepted; an open conversation is heard as it is written, with unread counts, "is writing" and "Seen". Built behind the gates; Contact on a solution page leads to Messages; waits for a walk through on staging with two accounts.
- [BEY-190 — The events of the GenAI Fund calendar on Luma come onto the site](increments/active/bey-190-luma-events/design.md): every hour the events still to come of `luma.com/gaf` become published programs of type event with their cover, days and Luma page, without the paid API; the home page's events card lists the next two events of the published programs instead of written text. Built and tested on 14 October 2026; waits for a look on staging, and for the Tasco demo day to be entered as an event before the release.
- [BEY-188 — The featured program is chosen in Admin](increments/active/bey-188-featured-program-from-admin/design.md): an operator features a program from Admin › Programs, and the home page's announcement shows the most recently featured program still taking applications, with its cover, words and dates read from the program instead of the code. Built and tested on 14 October 2026; waits for a look on staging and for Nestlé's early briefings to be entered as a key date before the release.
- [BEY-66 — The operators' review of solutions](increments/active/bey-66-admin-solution-review/design.md): the queue finds by organization, narrows by industry, names who sent each solution and says how many wait; the record reads as labelled rows with the decision in view. Backend verified; the web screens are checked by CI and still to be walked in the browser.
- [BEY-136 — Lists: page size, a country filter for operators, one search field per surface](increments/active/bey-136-list-controls/design.md): every paged list takes a page size, Admin › Organizations narrows by country, and the public directories search by meaning through one field. Built on 11 October 2026; the Figma frames are still to draw.
- [BEY-133 — Proposals to a use case](increments/active/bey-133-use-case-proposals/design.md): a provider sends a proposal to a published use case in three steps, and the members of the organization that posted it read what it received.
- [BEY-148 — A solution comes with offers](increments/active/bey-148-solution-offers/design.md): a solution's card says its offer in a line, from GenAI Fund's list at `/admin/offers` or from the organization's own.
- [BEY-158 — An offer is closed to new solutions](increments/active/bey-158-offer-close-and-record/design.md): an offer is open or closed, and an operator reads which solutions come with it.
