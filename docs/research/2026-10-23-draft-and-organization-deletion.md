# Deleting a draft and an organization that holds nothing

Read on 23 October 2026 for [BEY-246](https://linear.app/beyondpilot/issue/BEY-246/an-applicant-deletes-their-draft-and-an-organization-that-holds). That day an applicant who had tried the application form as a test team could not switch to his real company. Four rules stood in his way:

- Starting an application as an individual or a team creates an active organization the person owns (`OrganizationService.createForApplicant`).
- A person belongs to one organization at a time (`ORGANIZATION_ALREADY_MEMBER`).
- The last owner cannot leave (`ORGANIZATION_LAST_OWNER`).
- Nothing lets an applicant delete a draft, or an owner delete an organization.

His records were deleted by hand on production (recorded on BEY-242). This note records how other products handle the same questions. It decides nothing.

- **Codebases cloned under `.tmp/`:** decidim, pretalx, eventyay, pretix, indico, dub, saleor, wagtail, immich, and payload's documentation. The clones of discourse, supabase and devpost hold no relevant code, and medusa has no organization membership.

## Deleting a draft

Every codebase that has drafts lets their author delete one while it is a draft and only then. After submission the only way out is withdraw, which keeps the record.

## Belonging to organizations, leaving the last one, deleting one

Every product read lets a person belong to several organizations; BeyondPilot's one at a time is the outlier. Nearly every product refuses to let the last owner simply leave and offers two ways out: hand ownership over, or delete. Deleting is gated on what the organization holds or owes, not on who asks.

## When the team exists

- pretalx creates the speaker with the draft (`submission/domain/submission.py:93-94`), but co-speakers stay email addresses on the draft (`draft_additional_speakers`, `models/submission.py:222-226`). They become invitations only at submission (`domain/submission.py:135-152`).
- decidim adds a coauthor only when the invitee accepts (`invite_coauthor.rb:22-30`).
- On Devpost the team belongs to the project; teammates are added in the submission form ([121](https://help.devpost.com/article/121-teams-adding-teammates)).
- Devfolio appears to let a person apply alone and join a team later (from search snippets, unconfirmed).

No product read makes a lasting organization out of someone trying a form.

## Account deletion, for BEY-242

- decidim anonymises the user and deletes related records (`decidim-core/app/commands/decidim/destroy_account.rb:19-56`).
- pretalx deactivates, anonymises and drops team memberships (`person/domain/user.py:50-72`).
- dub refuses while the user owns a workspace: "You must transfer ownership of your workspaces or delete them before you can delete your account." (`app/api/user/route.ts:229-240`).
- immich soft-deletes with a 7-day grace period an administrator can restore (`services/user-admin.service.ts:98-125`).

## What this suggests for BeyondPilot

These are findings, not decisions; the design of BEY-246 decides.

1. **A draft is its author's to delete, while it is a draft.** Deleting it hard, with its files, is what decidim and pretalx do. Submitted applications keep Withdraw. Every reference asks for a confirmation. The team's rule of Undo over confirmation fits only if the deletion waits for the Undo to lapse.
2. **An organization that holds nothing can be deleted by its owner.** "Nothing" means:
   - no other member and no open invitation;
   - no submitted application or proposal;
   - no solution, use case or profile that is listed or in review.

   Otherwise the action is refused, with a list of what blocks it (Neon, Vercel, pretix) and a way to reach GenAI Fund for a merge or take-down. When the last owner of an empty organization leaves, it is deleted, as on Figma and Toggl. pretix logs such a deletion because an organization outlives one person.

3. **The organization could wait for submission.** pretalx keeps co-speakers on the draft and invites them at submission, and Devpost keeps the team with the project. Holding the team's name and size on the draft, and creating or joining the organization when the application is submitted, would mean a draft never takes the applicant's one organization. Points 1 and 2 are still needed for the organizations that already exist.
4. **The one-organization rule is the outlier.** Every product read allows several. Relaxing it is a larger product question than BEY-246; points 1 to 3 remove the trap without it.
