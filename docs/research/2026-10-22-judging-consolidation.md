# Judging consolidation: how judges' scores are read side by side

Read on 22 October 2026 for [BEY-241](https://linear.app/beyondpilot/issue/BEY-241), after the Tasco deliberation showed that an operator reads each judge's scores only by opening one application at a time. The earlier note on [review and judging](2026-10-06-review-and-judging.md) covers how a judge scores and how outcomes are released; this one covers how an organizer reads every judge's scores together.

## Disagreement between judges

- Consensus steps in procurement scoring flag a criterion where evaluators diverged by **2 points or more** for discussion ([RFP scoring matrix](https://claudepluginhub.com/skills/mohitagw15856-pm-supplychain-plugins-pm-supplychain/rfp-scoring-matrix)).
- A study of engineering design juries flags a score outside **1.5 standard deviations** of the jury's scores as a possible outlier ([ASEE](https://peer.asee.org/22467.pdf)).
- Normalization is said to mislead when scores do not follow the assumed distribution, and z-scores handle outliers better than min–max ([scoreNormalization](https://github.com/OpenTechStrategies/scoreNormalization)). With two to five judges, as at Tasco, a judge's standard deviation rests on a handful of scores.

## What BeyondPilot takes

- A **Scores** tab beside Applications, read only: scoring stays on the application page, which already lists every judge's scores by criterion, as Award Force's score matrix does.
- **By application** as the main view, ranked by the mean across judges (BeyondPilot's average already is a mean), each judge a column, with how many scored it.
- **By judge** as a second view, as Evessio's: what each judge scored, stepped back from and their mean, so that a strict or a lenient judge shows without normalizing.
- The **spread** between the highest and lowest judge on an application, flagged at 2 points on the 1 to 10 scale, and sortable, so that a deliberation can start from where judges disagree. A standard deviation over two or three judges says less than the spread and is harder to read.
- An **export with one line per judge and application**, every criterion's score, the note and whether it was a conflict: the Score by Judge sheet each product has.
- **Not now:** normalization, a consensus score a head judge overrides, weighted criteria.
