# Programs and events

Collected on 2 October 2026 for the Programs and events pages, from the sources GenAI Fund named: the [blog](https://genaifund.ai/blog/) (90 posts, 23 of them in the events category) and the Luma calendars [genaifund](https://luma.com/genaifund?period=past) and [gaf](https://luma.com/gaf?compact=true) (56 events). The AI for Insurance Challenge is on neither and comes from its [campaign page](https://beyondpilot.genaifund.ai/insurance-ai-tasco). Every one of the 56 Luma events is attached to a program. Statuses are as of 2 October 2026. The full records are in [data/programs-and-events.json](data/programs-and-events.json).

## What this means for the design

- **There are two kinds.** A program with applications (challenge, open innovation call, accelerator, hackathon, grant) uses BeyondPilot's application flow. An event that people only register for (meetup, workshop, webinar) keeps its registration on Luma, and BeyondPilot links to it.
- **A program contains events.** Agentic AI Build Week has 19 and Shinhan Innoboost has 4 (a webinar, office hours, a demo day). A program page lists the events inside it.
- **GenAI Open Innovation runs per country** (Vietnam, Japan, Malaysia, Indonesia): one call, many enterprises.
- **One program is open today**, the AI for Insurance Challenge. The only upcoming events are the GenAI Builders Meetups of 13 October, 17 November and 15 December 2026; Luma lists no venue for them yet.
- **A recurring event is one item** with several dates, not one item per date.
- **Covers.** Most programs have a cover on Luma, but many posters use purple, which the product's palette rejects, so photographs from the blog recaps are preferred.
- **A gap.** "Nestlé Vietnam AI Reinvention" appears only as a tag on the old platform, with no blog post or Luma event.
