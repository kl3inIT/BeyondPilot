# The live site on 5 October 2026

GenAI Fund's own BeyondPilot site, [beyondpilot.genaifund.ai](https://beyondpilot.genaifund.ai/), was read again on 5 October 2026, together with the two subdomains its home page links to. The note records what the repository had not yet written down; earlier readings are in [existing products](2026-10-02-existing-products.md) and the [main-flows research](../increments/active/bey-27-main-flows-design/research.md). Whether the home page already existed before 1 October is not known: earlier notes cover only the campaign page.

**Method.** Every route found in the site's sitemap and in its JavaScript bundle was rendered at 1440 and 390 px wide in Chrome, without signing in. The visible text of each page is kept in [captures/live-site/](captures/live-site/). Nothing was submitted.

Two statements on the home page disagree with the campaign page:

- The programme collection labels the challenge **Upcoming**, while the campaign page says "Live now" and applications opened on 23 September.
- Its card describes the challenge as "Make insurance renewal simpler for customers and more effective for operations teams", the framing of the live form's challenge areas, while the campaign page states an open scope across buying, carrying and claiming.

## AI talent

`aitalent.genaifund.ai` asks for sign-in on every page except these:

- [Jobs](https://aitalent.genaifund.ai/jobs): a builder job board filtered by country, employment and work mode, with three roles marked "Seeded roles".
- [People](https://aitalent.genaifund.ai/jobs/people): public builder profiles from the build week; none were listed.
- [Report 2026](https://aitalent.genaifund.ai/report-2026): "State of AI Builders in Southeast Asia", a public preview drawn from 2,719 approved builders in 55 countries, with the full report on request.
- [Register](https://aitalent.genaifund.ai/register): personal details, job title, company, company type, industry, country, LinkedIn, years of AI/ML experience, AI models and tools, programming languages.

Sign-in takes Google, an email and password, or an emailed link.
