Before you use the BeyondPilot platform, please read how we handle your personal data. Your agreement is required to access the platform.

## 1. Who we are

BeyondPilot is funded and operated by GenAI Fund, through GenAI Technology Development Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us"). You can contact us at any time through the BeyondPilot platform.

## 2. What data we collect

- Account details: name, email, phone number, organization and job title.
- Profile and program details: company or startup information, submissions, pitch materials, evaluations and feedback.
- Usage data: login activity, pages viewed (with your cookie consent), and device and browser information.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data. Please do not upload it to the platform.

## 3. Why we use it

- To create and manage your account and give you access to the platform.
- To run BeyondPilot programs, including matching, judging, mentoring and communication with participants.
- To share relevant information with program partners, such as partner enterprises, judges and mentors, when needed for the program.
- With your agreement, to use AI to suggest your profile for relevant opportunities and to help evaluate your applications. People make the decisions.
- To improve the platform and keep it secure.

## 4. Who we share it with

We only share your data with program partners, with service providers who help us run the platform, and with authorities when required by law. Your data may be stored or processed outside Vietnam, with appropriate safeguards in place.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active, and for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. After that, we delete or anonymize it.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict how it is used, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before the withdrawal, but some features may no longer be available to you.

## 7. Your consent

- I have read and agree to BeyondPilot processing my personal data to use the platform and take part in its programs. (Required)
- I agree to BeyondPilot sharing my information with program partners for matching and evaluation. (Required to take part in programs)
- I agree to BeyondPilot using AI to suggest my profile for relevant opportunities and to help evaluate my applications. (Optional)
- I agree to receive updates, newsletters and event invitations from BeyondPilot. (Optional)

This consent is provided in line with Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 91/2025/QH15) and Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP.
